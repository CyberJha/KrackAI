## 1. Executive Summary

**Researchub** is an autonomous multi-agent deep research and forensic linguistic humanization platform. It solves a critical bottleneck in computer-assisted academia and enterprise publishing: **the statistical signature of Large Language Models (LLMs).**

While mainstream AI generates content with predictable token n-grams and uniform sentence lengths, modern forensic neural classifiers (Turnitin v2026, GPTZero, ZeroGPT, CopyLeaks) identify and flag these synthetic markers. Researchub operates a **5-Agent Autonomous Research Pipeline** coupled with the proprietary **PR-39 Linguistic Evasion Protocol (31 Rules & 10 Humanizing Directives)**. It guarantees **100% factual invariance** while completely restructuring sentence cadence, burstiness, and perplexity to achieve a **0% AI Risk score**.

## 2. Core Problem & Academic Need

1. **The "Synthetic Tone" Barrier:** Standard LLM outputs rely heavily on uniform phrasing, pseudo-depth maxims, formulaic tripartite structures, and repetitive transition words (e.g., *"Furthermore"*, *"Delve"*, *"It is worth noting"*).
2. **False Positives & Detector Penalties:** Academic institutions and publishers deploy multi-classifier detector arrays. Uncalibrated drafts risk rejection, academic integrity flags, or publication disqualification.
3. **The Hallucination Dilemma in Current Humanizers:** Existing commercial humanizers use blunt synonym-swapping ("spinners") that distort citations, alter technical claims, and corrupt statistical constants.
4. **The Researchub Solution:** Researchub couples multi-agent retrieval with strict **Factual Invariance Locks**. Every numerical constant, claim boundary, and citation is preserved with cryptographic precision, while the syntactic cadence is dynamically modulated.

## 3. Product Architecture & 3 Operating Modes

Researchub operates through a unified, glassmorphic Command Center with three specialized workflows:

### Mode 1: Multi-Agent Deep Research (`Alt+1`)
- **5-Agent Autonomous Pipeline:**
  1. *Thesis Decomposition Manager:* Deconstructs complex research inquiries into hypothesis trees.
  2. *Retrieval & Citation Extraction Node:* Pulls verified academic citations and domain-specific evidence.
  3. *Technical Synthesis Writer:* Drafts rigorous academic manuscripts.
  4. *Fact Critic & Invariance Auditor:* Validates factual accuracy against source material.
  5. *PR-39 Humanizer Node:* Inverts burstiness, modulates entropy, and purges syntactic AI signatures.
- **Output:** Publication-grade Markdown research briefs with integrated bibliography and zero-risk forensic validation.

### Mode 2: PR-39 Humanizer Studio (`Alt+2`)
- **10 Humanizing Directives:** Configurable prompt rulesets (conversational grounding, burstiness modulation, sensory anchors, avatar-tailored perspective).
- **Interactive Before & After Matrix:** Live comparative breakdown of sentences across the 31 PR-39 rules (e.g., False Contrast Purge, Forced Trios Excision, AI Buzzword Removal).
- **Live Cadence Visualizer:** Graphic inspection of sentence-length distribution and rhythm variance.

### Mode 3: Forensic AI Classifier Auditor (`Alt+3`)
- **4-Detector Neural Cross-Validation Array:** Real-time scoring simulating:
  - **Turnitin Academic Classifier** (v2026 Engine)
  - **GPTZero Enterprise**
  - **ZeroGPT Neural Analyzer**
  - **CopyLeaks Deep Scanner**
- **Forensic Metrics:**
  - *Perplexity Standard:* Target log-likelihood entropy > 3.8.
  - *Cadence Burstiness:* Sentence length standard deviation > 12.4 words (Score > 85/100).
  - *Lexical Slop Marker Count:* 0 flagged AI transition patterns.

## 4. Proprietary Technical Assets

- **The PR-39 Protocol (31 Rules):** Structured into 6 authoritative groups:
  - *Group A (Staging):* Removes artificial scene-setting and false contrast ("It's not X, it's Y").
  - *Group B (Formulaic Rhythm):* Breaks forced tripartite clauses and repetitive sentence openers.
  - *Group C (Authority):* Eliminates hyperbolic significance clichés and superficial praise.
  - *Group D (Typesetting):* Eliminates generic AI bullet styles and formatting crutches.
  - *Group E (Chat Remnants):* Eliminates conversational artifacts ("Certainly!", "Here is a breakdown").
  - *Group F (Multilingual/Translation Nuances):* Normalizes idioms and cultural cadence.
- **Client-Side Security:** Zero backend credential storage; all API provider keys (Gemini, Groq, OpenAI) remain strictly local in browser `localStorage`.
- **Factual Invariance Guarantee:** A hard system constraint guaranteeing that dates, statistics, proper nouns, and uncertainty degrees remain unchanged during humanization passes.
